## Pages

### Home page section order (hero → bottom)

1. **Hero** — first impression: name, "I turn ideas into realities.", profile photo, primary CTAs (Get in touch / Download CV).
2. **About me** — numbered cards answering who I am / what I do / how I work / why it works, plus a staggered photo collage.
3. **What I can do for you** — the four R&D services in an autoplaying neobrutalism carousel with a per-slide progress bar (product development, formulation & process optimization, production & QA, analytical development).
4. **Featured Projects** — proof of work (top 3) with a "View all projects" link to `/projects`.
5. **Testimonials** — one social-proof section: "Over 70+ people trust me" quote-card grid (Read more/less per card) + WhatsApp review screenshots as an auto-scrolling two-row marquee with click-to-zoom lightbox ("What they said").
6. **Footer** — closing contact CTA (email, Instagram, LinkedIn, address).

Rationale: hook → who → offer → proof → validation → act.

### Menu

`Home · About · Projects · Blog`

- **Home** (`/`) — landing page with the section order above.
- **About** (`/about`) — personal bio and CV.
- **Projects** (`/projects`) — full list of cosmetic projects; the home page shows a featured subset with a "View all" link.
- **Blog** (`/blog`) — articles.
